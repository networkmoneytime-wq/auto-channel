Privacy Policy

auto-channel is operated by a single individual solely to manage their own social media accounts. It is not a public-facing service and does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any third party.

The app connects to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram using OAuth credentials that authorize it to act only on the operator's own accounts, using each platform's official API. Access tokens are stored locally/in private repository secrets and are never shared with third parties.

No end-user data is collected because the app has no end users other than its own operator.

Contact: see the repository for contact information.
